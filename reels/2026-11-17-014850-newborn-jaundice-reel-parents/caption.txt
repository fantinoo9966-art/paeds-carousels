👶💛 Is your newborn turning yellow? Here's when it's normal and when it's NOT.

✅ About 6 in 10 full-term babies get jaundice. It usually shows on day 2–3 and fades by 2 weeks.

🟢 Usually normal: starts after 24 hours, baby feeds well and is active, fades by 2 weeks.
🔴 Not normal: yellow in the first 24 hours, spreading to legs, palms and soles, or lasting beyond 2 weeks.

💚 What really helps:
1️⃣ Feed often: 8–12 times a day (breastfeeding is best)
2️⃣ Count wet nappies: 6 or more a day from day 5
3️⃣ Get baby checked within 2–3 days of going home
4️⃣ Phototherapy if your doctor advises: it is safe and works well

❌ Please don't:
• Put baby in direct sunlight
• Give water or glucose water
• Stop breastfeeding
• Try home remedies on the skin

🚨 See a doctor URGENTLY if:
• Yellow in the first 24 hours
• Very sleepy, hard to wake, not feeding
• High-pitched cry or arching back
• Pale or white stools, dark yellow urine
• Yellow palms and soles
• Still yellow after 2 weeks (3 weeks if premature)
