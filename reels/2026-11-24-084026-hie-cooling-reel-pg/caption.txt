🧊🧠 HIE & therapeutic cooling | PG Notes · Neonatology 05

🩸 Evidence of asphyxia: cord/1st-hour gas pH ≤ 7.0 or base deficit ≥ 16 · Apgar ≤ 5 at 10 min or ongoing resuscitation · plus moderate–severe encephalopathy

📊 Sarnat staging:
• Stage 1 (mild): hyperalert, irritable, dilated pupils, no seizures
• Stage 2 (moderate): lethargic, hypotonia, constricted pupils, seizures common
• Stage 3 (severe): stupor/coma, flaccid, absent reflexes, highest mortality

✅ Who to cool: ≥ 36 weeks · start within 6 h of birth · moderate/severe encephalopathy ± abnormal aEEG · unit with ventilation & monitoring

🌡️ Protocol: core 33.5 °C (33–34) for 72 h → rewarm 0.5 °C/hour, watch for seizures

🛟 Supportive: avoid hyperthermia & hypocarbia · normal glucose · maintain BP · seizures: phenobarbitone 20 mg/kg · watch for SIADH, AKI · MRI after rewarming

⭐ Pearls:
• Pupils dilated in stage 1, constricted in stage 2
• HELIX trial (2021): cooling in low/middle-income settings did not reduce death or disability: follow unit policy
• Not standard for < 36 weeks outside trials

📚 Ref: NICE/BAPM, TOBY, NICHD, HELIX trials
