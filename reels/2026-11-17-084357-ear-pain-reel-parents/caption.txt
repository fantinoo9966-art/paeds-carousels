👂😣 Ear pain at night? Here's what to do (and what NOT to do).

✅ Most ear infections get better in about 3 days. Many don't need antibiotics: your doctor decides.

💚 What helps:
1️⃣ Paracetamol or ibuprofen for pain, in the dose your doctor advises
2️⃣ Keep feeding & give fluids
3️⃣ Lots of rest and cuddles
4️⃣ Doctor check if not better in 2–3 days

❌ Please don't:
• Put oil or home drops in the ear
• Poke with earbuds or pins
• Give leftover antibiotics
• Bottle-feed lying flat

🚨 See a doctor URGENTLY if:
• Pus or blood coming from the ear
• Swelling or redness behind the ear
• Very unwell, high fever, stiff neck
• Baby under 6 months with ear pain
• Pain not better after 2–3 days
• Trouble hearing that doesn't go away

🛡️ Prevent: breastfeed · vaccines on time · no smoking near kids · feed baby upright · wash hands
