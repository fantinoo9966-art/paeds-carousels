🍼⚠️ NEC: modified Bell staging | PG Notes · Neonatology 06

⚡ Risk factors: prematurity (biggest) · formula feeds · rapid feed advance · asphyxia/hypoxia · absent/reversed EDF · polycythaemia · exchange transfusion · umbilical catheters

🩺 Signs: feed intolerance with rising gastric aspirates · abdominal distension, tenderness · bilious vomiting · blood in stools · apnoea, lethargy, temperature instability

📊 Modified Bell staging:
• Stage I (suspected): systemic + mild GI signs; X-ray normal or mild ileus
• Stage II (definite): pneumatosis intestinalis ± portal venous gas
• Stage III (advanced): shock, DIC, peritonitis; pneumoperitoneum = IIIB

💊 Management:
• NPO + gastric decompression; TPN; NPO about 7–14 days in stage II
• Broad-spectrum IV antibiotics, e.g. ampicillin + gentamicin + metronidazole
• Surgery: perforation (IIIB) or clinical deterioration

⭐ Pearls: pneumatosis intestinalis = hallmark of stage II · football / Rigler sign = pneumoperitoneum · breast milk protects; probiotics reduce NEC in preterms

📚 Ref: Walsh & Kliegman modified Bell staging, Nelson
