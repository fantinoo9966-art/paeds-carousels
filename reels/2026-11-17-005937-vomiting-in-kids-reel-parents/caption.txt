🤢 Your child is vomiting… again? Here's exactly what to do.

✅ Good news: most vomiting is a tummy bug (viral) and usually stops in 1–2 days.

🍼 Spit-up or vomit?
• Spit-up: small amount, comes up easily, happy growing baby → usually normal
• Vomit: forceful, large amount, child looks unwell → watch closely

💧 The real risk is DEHYDRATION. Give small sips:
1️⃣ 1 teaspoon (5 ml) of ORS every 1–2 minutes
2️⃣ Vomited? Wait 10 minutes, then start again slowly
3️⃣ Babies: keep breastfeeding, short and often
4️⃣ No vomiting for 4–6 hours? Slowly restart normal food

❌ Please don't:
• Give anti-vomiting medicines without a doctor
• Give big gulps of water
• Give soft drinks, juice or glucose water
• Stop breastfeeding

🚨 See a doctor URGENTLY if:
• Green or bloody vomit
• Severe tummy pain or a swollen tummy
• Very sleepy, hard to wake
• No urine for 6–8 hours, dry mouth, no tears
• Vomiting after a head injury
• Baby under 3 months, or forceful (projectile) vomiting
• Vomiting more than 24 hours (baby) or more than 2 days (child)
