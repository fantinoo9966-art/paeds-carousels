🤧 Cough & cold… AGAIN? Here's what really helps (and what doesn't).

✅ Good news: most colds get better on their own in 7–10 days. A cough can last 2–3 weeks.
🦠 Colds are caused by viruses, so antibiotics DON'T help.

💚 What really helps:
1️⃣ Saline nose drops (+ gentle suction for babies)
2️⃣ Honey ½–1 tsp at bedtime, ONLY above 1 year
3️⃣ Warm fluids & keep feeding
4️⃣ Lots of rest
5️⃣ Paracetamol for fever, in the dose your doctor advises

❌ Please don't:
• Cough & cold syrups under 4 years (unless your doctor prescribes)
• Honey under 1 year
• Hot steam near kids (burns!)
• Vapour rub in or under the nose

🏥 See a doctor if:
• Fast breathing or chest pulling in
• Wheeze or noisy breathing
• Blue or grey lips
• Not feeding, fewer wet diapers
• Fever more than 3 days (any fever under 3 months)
• Ear pain, or cough beyond 3 weeks
