🫁 That whistling sound when your child breathes is a wheeze. About 1 in 3 children wheeze before age 3, and many outgrow it: not every wheeze is asthma.

🏠 Triggers live at home:
• Kitchen: tadka & chulha smoke → keep your child away from cooking smoke
• Bedroom: mosquito coil, dusty pillows & mites → use nets, wash bedsheets weekly in hot water
• Living room: agarbatti, cigarette smoke, pets → no smoking or agarbatti indoors
• Outside: cold air, colds & viruses → cover nose & mouth in cold air, wash hands

💨 If your child has an inhaler: use the spacer every time · preventer every day, even when well · reliever for attacks, as the doctor planned · rinse mouth after the steroid inhaler

❌ Please don't: give cough syrups for a wheeze · stop the preventer when well · smoke near your child · use hot steam (burns!)

🚨 Go to hospital NOW if: struggling to breathe, can't talk or feed · chest pulling in, ribs showing · blue or grey lips · reliever not working or needed every 4 hours · very sleepy or confused
