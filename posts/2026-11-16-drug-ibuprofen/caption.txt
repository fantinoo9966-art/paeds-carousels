From viral fever in OPD to closing a preterm PDA in the NICU—Ibuprofen wears multiple hats! 💊

NEET-PG & Ward Pearls:
• Ensure adequate hydration before IV dosing to lower renal risk.
• In preterm PDA (<32 wk), hold the 2nd or 3rd dose if urine output falls below 0.6 mL/kg/hr.
• Contraindicated in active GI bleed, NEC, thrombocytopenia, or duct-dependent congenital heart disease.
• Causes less severe renal side effects than IV indomethacin for PDA closure.
