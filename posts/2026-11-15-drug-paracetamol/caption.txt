For NEET-PG & ward rounds, remember three key points: Paracetamol lacks anti-inflammatory activity, neonatal IV max doses depend on gestational and postnatal age, and it is used for PDA closure when NSAIDs fail or are contraindicated. Watch for hepatotoxicity with CYP2E1 inducers and never exceed 5 PO/PR doses in 24 hours.
