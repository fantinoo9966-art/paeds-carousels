Why do we double the amoxicillin dose in paediatric pneumonia and acute otitis media? 💊

In OPDs and NEET-PG exams, knowing standard vs high-dose amoxicillin is essential. High-dose therapy (80–90 mg/kg/day) with 8-hourly dosing maximises time above MIC to overcome penicillin-resistant Streptococcus pneumoniae. Remember: it achieves roughly double the serum levels of oral ampicillin with fewer GI side effects, and requires renal dose adjustment!
