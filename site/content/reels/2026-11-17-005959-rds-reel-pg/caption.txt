🫁 RDS & surfactant: everything in one Reel | PG Notes · Neonatology 03

🧪 Why: surfactant deficiency → ↑ surface tension → alveoli collapse (Laplace P = 2T/r) → atelectasis, hyaline membranes
• Made by type II pneumocytes · main lipid DPPC · proteins SP-B, SP-C · mature by ~34–35 wk

⬆️ Risk up: prematurity · male · maternal diabetes · C-section without labour · perinatal asphyxia · 2nd twin
⬇️ Risk down: antenatal steroids · chronic stress (PIH, IUGR) · prolonged PROM

🩺 Clinical: onset < 4–6 h · tachypnoea > 60, grunting, flaring, retractions, cyanosis · worst 48–72 h · scores: Silverman-Anderson, Downes

🩻 X-ray: ground-glass (reticulogranular) · air bronchograms · low lung volumes · grade IV = white-out

💉 Antenatal steroids at 24–34 wk: betamethasone 12 mg IM × 2 (24 h apart) or dexamethasone 6 mg IM 12-hourly × 4

🏥 Delivery room: delayed cord clamping ≥ 60 s · CPAP from birth ≥ 6 cm H₂O · start FiO₂ 0.30 (< 28 wk), 0.21–0.30 (28–31 wk)

💧 Surfactant:
• Rescue if FiO₂ > 0.30 on CPAP ≥ 6 cm H₂O and worsening
• Poractant 200 mg/kg (repeat 100 mg/kg) · Beractant 100 mg/kg (4 ml/kg)
• LISA/MIST preferred over INSURE in spontaneously breathing babies
• 2nd/3rd dose if O₂ need persists

🛟 Supportive: caffeine · SpO₂ target 90–94% · fluids 70–80 ml/kg/day · antibiotics until sepsis excluded (GBS pneumonia mimics RDS)

🔎 Differentials: TTN · GBS pneumonia · MAS · cyanotic CHD · pneumothorax
⚠️ Complications: air leak · BPD · IVH · PDA · ROP

⭐ Exam pearls:
• L/S ratio ≥ 2 = mature lungs
• Phosphatidylglycerol present = mature
• Shake (bubble) test on gastric aspirate

📚 Ref: European Consensus Guidelines on the Management of RDS (2022)
